I hold a Master’s degree in **Software, Business Processes, and Database Engineering**, earned at the

Prior to this, I completed my Bachelor's degree in **Aerospace and Satellite Engineering** at the

## Setup

I use **Arch Linux** as my main operating system. In my opinion, it provides a much better environment for 
software development compared to Windows due to its flexibility and community-driven nature.
Although I’ve considered trying MacOS in the future, I haven’t had the chance to try it yet.

Previously, I utilized the **i3 window manager**, but I recently switched to **vanilla GNOME**. 
Its simplicity, user-friendly design, and ability to work seamlessly "out of the box" won me over. 
For those interested, my configuration files can be found in my 
[public dotfiles repository](https://github.com/Ganji00/dotfiles-git) on GitHub.


## Work

At my current position, in addition to other responsibilities, I have the opportunity to collaboratively manage 
(with my amazing team) the **on-premises infrastructure** for our internal solutions. 
This has been an incredible learning experience, as it allowed me to deepen my knowledge in 
**Infrastructure as Code (IaC)**, Linux administration, and DevOps practices.

Switching entirely to **Linux** on my personal computer was undoubtedly influenced by managing Linux systems at work 
and by the guidance of my team leader, a true Linux enthusiast. Thanks to his mentorship, 
I’ve grown to appreciate the flexibility, power, and elegance of Linux systems.

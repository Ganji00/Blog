## Setup

I use Arch Linux (BTW) as my main operating system. I made this choice as, in my opinion, it's much easier to do
software development on it than on Windows. I would love to try MacOS, but I don't have a MacBook yet.
I used to run i3 window manager, but recently I have switched to vanilla Gnome. I love the simplicity of it and the 
fact that it works out of the box. For intrested, there is a public dotfiles repository on 
my [Github](https://github.com/Ganji00/dotfiles-git) with my configuration.
